About this project

What the data are, how they were processed, and what they cannot tell you.

Data

53 monthly reports published by the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) on how Central Ministries/Departments handle grievances on CPGRAMS (May 2022 – Sep 2026). 36,718 values were extracted from the PDFs; each keeps its report, page and table. 92 ministries and departments appear under 121 printed names.

Twelve randomly sampled values were checked against the PDF pages (12 of 12 match), and the whole pipeline was re-run from the PDFs into a fresh database with identical results. These checks were done by an AI assistant at the project owner's request, not by an independent person.

Methods

  • Each month's figure comes from the latest report that prints it; partial months are flagged.
  • Trends use the Mann–Kendall test with an autocorrelation correction, Sen's slope for size, and false-discovery control across ministries.
  • Lists are compared only within one threshold, rankings only within one method era and group, and absence from a list is never treated as zero.
  • The scorecard is a project construct, not an official rating, and is always shown with its 90% rank interval.

The complaint assistant

The assistant suggests a department using the official Allocation of Business Rules (Cabinet Secretariat) and project-written keywords, asks a question when it is unsure, and drafts a complaint using only what you wrote. On 300 synthetic test complaints it chose the right department first 65% of the time and had it in its top three 89% of the time, which is why it always shows alternatives. You submit complaints yourself on the official CPGRAMS portal.

What this cannot tell you

  • Why a backlog rose or fell: the data are observational.
  • How your own complaint will be handled: these are aggregate figures.
  • Anything about State Government grievances: only Central Ministries/Departments are covered.

Grievance Lens is an independent portfolio project, not a government website. It is not affiliated with DARPG or CPGRAMS and never submits complaints.
