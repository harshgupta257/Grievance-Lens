Privacy policy

What happens to what you type, in plain words.

In short

  • This site does not store your complaint. It has no database of complaints, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.
  • It never submits anything to the government. You copy the draft and submit it yourself on the official CPGRAMS portal.
  • Please do not type your name, address, phone number, Aadhaar or bank details. The draft leaves placeholders for anything personal.

What you type in the complaint assistant

  • Your complaint and the details you add are sent to this site's server to suggest a department and build a draft. They are processed in memory and discarded when the response is sent.
  • The server's logs record only the page or action requested, the result code and the time taken, never the text you typed.
  • To limit abuse, your IP address is kept in memory for about a minute to count requests. The hosting provider also keeps standard access logs (IP address, page and time) under its own policy.
  • The draft exists only in your browser tab until you copy it. Closing the tab removes it.

AI provider

Checking which AI provider is in use…

By default no AI service is used: the department is suggested by this site's own search and rules, and nothing leaves the server. If an AI provider is switched on, the complaint is sent to it to choose between the suggested departments, after these steps:

  • Phone numbers, Aadhaar-like numbers, PAN, e-mail addresses, bank account and card numbers and IFSC codes are replaced with placeholders before sending. Names and addresses written in free text are not detected, which is why you should leave them out.
  • Only the complaint and the candidate departments are sent, never your other details.
  • Only paid API plans are used, under which the provider does not use the text to train or improve its models. Providers keep requests for a limited time to detect abuse.
  • To save cost, the AI's answer (the department chosen and a short reason) is kept on the server so that an identical complaint gets the same answer without a second call. The reason can paraphrase your complaint. This cache is deleted whenever the server restarts.

The statistics pages

They show published, aggregate figures about ministries. They contain no information about any individual complainant.
