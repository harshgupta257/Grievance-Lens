Grievance Lens

An independent look at how Central Government ministries handle public grievances, from 53 monthly DARPG reports, plus an assistant that helps you prepare a complaint.

Grievance Lens is an independent portfolio project, not a government website. It is not affiliated with DARPG or CPGRAMS and never submits complaints.
